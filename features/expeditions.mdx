Expeditions let Pokémon from your PC take on timed missions while you continue your own journey — your team's strengths affect success rates and rewards. Rather than leaving your benched Pokémon idle, you can dispatch them on meaningful assignments that return items, experience, and progression even when you're busy exploring elsewhere.

## Sending Your First Team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Right-click an Expedition Board">
    Interact with an Expedition Board to open the mission browser. Boards appear at key locations around the server — find one and get familiar with its interface before your first dispatch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse missions and review the details">
    Each mission shows its hazards, success odds, cost, and expected duration. Read these carefully and select a mission that suits the Pokémon you plan to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Pokémon from your PC">
    Choose which Pokémon to dispatch from your PC storage. The board displays each Pokémon's contribution to the mission's odds as you build your team, so adjust your selection until you're satisfied with the projected outcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait, then collect your rewards">
    Confirm the dispatch and go about your adventure. When the mission timer expires, return to the same Expedition Board to claim whatever your team brought back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Team Expedition Power (TEP)

**Team Expedition Power** measures how well your chosen Pokémon suit the mission at hand. A high TEP translates directly into better success odds and richer rewards. Several factors feed into TEP:

- **Pokémon types** — matching the mission's environment or challenge type boosts suitability
- **IVs and EVs** — stronger individual stats contribute to overall power
- **Team size** — filling all available slots generally improves your chances
- **Mission matchups** — some missions reward specific types or move categories more than others

Build diverse PC boxes with a range of types so you always have a well-matched team ready to send out.

## Expedition Outcomes

The result your team achieves determines what comes home with them:

| Outcome | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Great Success** | Maximum rewards; bonus loot possible |
| **Success** | Full reward set returned |
| **Partial Success** | Some rewards returned; mission goals partly met |
| **Failure** | Minimal or no rewards; Pokémon return safely |

Your Pokémon always come back — outcomes affect the loot, not your team.

## Tips for Better Expeditions

- **Match types to the mission** — review the mission details and select Pokémon whose types align with the listed hazards and environment.
- **Rotate your roster** — Pokémon accumulate fatigue after returning from a mission. Give them time to recover before dispatching them again.
- **Raise your expedition rank** — completing missions consistently unlocks higher-tier board listings with better rewards and new challenges.
- **Use augments** — available augment items can shift mission speed, alter reward tables, or improve your odds for specific mission types.

## Useful Commands

```
/expedition check    — view active expeditions, remaining time, and odds
/expedition rewards  — check pending rewards (collect them at the board)
```

<Note>
  Cost, duration, and reward details are shown per mission inside the board interface and depend on server settings. Always review them before dispatching to avoid surprises.
</Note>
