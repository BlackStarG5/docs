<h2>Adventure beyond your active team</h2>

<p>Expeditions let Pokémon from your <strong>PC</strong> take on timed missions while you continue your own journey. Your team’s strengths influence its chances and rewards.</p>

<h2>Send your first team</h2>

<ol><li><strong>Right-click an Expedition Board</strong> and browse the available missions.</li><li>Select Pokémon from your PC. Review the mission hazards and success odds before dispatching them.</li><li>Wait for the expedition to finish, then return to the board to collect its rewards.</li></ol>

<h2>Build a better expedition team</h2>

<p><strong>Team Expedition Power (TEP)</strong> measures your team’s suitability. Pokémon types, IVs, EVs, team size, and mission matchups all matter. A carefully chosen team can outperform one picked at random.</p>

<ul><li><strong>Outcomes:</strong> great success, success, partial success, and failure affect what comes back.</li><li><strong>Progression:</strong> expedition rank and board tiers open further opportunities.</li><li><strong>Fatigue:</strong> give returning Pokémon time to recover and rotate teams.</li><li><strong>Augments:</strong> available upgrades can change speed, rewards, or mission odds.</li></ul>

<h2>Useful commands</h2>

<p><code>/expedition check</code> — view active expeditions, remaining time, and odds.</p>

<p><code>/expedition rewards</code> — check pending rewards; collect them at the board.</p>

<p><strong>Before dispatching:</strong> read the displayed cost, duration, and reward information. These depend on the mission and server settings.</p>
