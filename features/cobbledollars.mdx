<h2>Your Pokémon adventure fund</h2>

<p><strong>CobbleDollars</strong> are an in-game currency used by merchants. Defeating wild Pokémon can award money, with the amount influenced by the Pokémon’s level and server settings.</p>

<h2>Buying from a merchant</h2>

<ol><li><strong>Right-click</strong> a merchant to open its shop.</li><li>Browse categories or search for an item.</li><li>Choose the quantity and check the total before buying.</li><li>If the price is red, you do not have enough money for that purchase.</li></ol>

<h2>Selling items</h2>

<p>Use <code>Shift + right-click</code> on a merchant to open its bank interface. Merchants only buy items included in their configured trade list, so check what is accepted and the offered price before selling.</p>

<h2>Keep track of your balance</h2>

<p>The money display helps you see what you can afford. Save up for something useful, and compare prices before spending your hard-earned balance.</p>

<p><strong>Realm reminder:</strong> your items and Pokémon carry between Hikari Haven’s realms, but <strong>money is separate between realms</strong>.</p>

<p><strong>Different currencies:</strong> do not assume CobbleDollars can replace arcade or gacha coins. Each activity may require its own currency; read the shop or machine’s instructions.</p>
