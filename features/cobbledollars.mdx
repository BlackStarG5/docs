CobbleDollars are the in-game currency used by merchants across Hikari Haven. Defeat wild Pokémon to earn money, then spend it on items and supplies. The amount you earn from each battle depends on the Pokémon's level and the server's settings, so stronger opponents tend to pay more.

## Buying from a Merchant

Find a merchant anywhere in the world and follow these steps to make a purchase.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the shop">
    Right-click a merchant to open its shop interface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your item">
    Browse the available categories or use the search bar to locate the item you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set quantity and review the total">
    Choose how many you want and check the total cost before confirming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check if you can afford it">
    If the price displays in red, you don't have enough CobbleDollars. Head out and earn more by battling wild Pokémon before returning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Selling Items

Use **Shift + right-click** on a merchant to open the bank interface. Merchants only buy items on their configured trade list, so check what is accepted and review the offered price before you sell. Not every item will appear in every merchant's buyback list.

## Checking Your Balance

Run `/balance` at any time to see how much you currently have. To send money to another player, use `/cobbledollars pay <player> <amount>` — handy for trades or helping out a friend who's just getting started.

<Warning>
  Your balance is separate between realms. Money earned in White Forest does not carry over to other realms. Check your balance after switching realms before making purchases.
</Warning>

<Info>
  CobbleDollars are different from Arcade Coins, Relic Coins, and Koban Coins. Each activity uses its own currency — read the shop or machine's instructions before spending to make sure you're using the right one.
</Info>
