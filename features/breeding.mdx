Breeding in Hikari Haven uses the Pasture Block system to produce eggs — a reliable path to building competitive teams and filling your Pokédex with species you've raised from scratch. By pairing the right Pokémon and equipping strategic held items, you can shape each new generation to inherit exactly the traits you need.

## Your First Egg

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place compatible Pokémon in a Pasture Block">
    Set up a Pasture Block and add two Pokémon to it. Keep in mind that sharing a pasture alone doesn't guarantee compatibility — the pair must meet the game's compatibility criteria before an egg can be produced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the breeding button">
    Open the pasture interface and click the breeding button to signal that the two Pokémon should attempt to breed. Watch the interface for confirmation that the pair is compatible and the process has started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for an egg to appear">
    Give it time. When the pair produces an egg, it appears in the nesting area at the bottom of the pasture interface. Check back periodically until you see it there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect the egg and keep it in your inventory">
    Pick up the egg and keep it in your **player inventory** so the hatching timer can run. Storing it in a chest, your PC, or anywhere outside your inventory pauses the timer until you retrieve it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Passing Down Traits

Plan which held items your Pokémon carry into the pasture — they directly control what the offspring inherits.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Everstone" icon="leaf" href="/features/breeding">
    The holder passes its **nature** down to the offspring. Equip it on whichever parent has the nature you want to preserve.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Destiny Knot" icon="link" href="/features/breeding">
    Causes **5 of the 12 combined IVs** from both parents to be inherited. Pair it with high-IV parents to stack the odds in your favour.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Power Items" icon="bolt" href="/features/breeding">
    Each Power Item guarantees the holder's **specific IV stat** passes to the offspring, letting you lock in individual stats one at a time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Egg Moves" icon="egg" href="/features/breeding">
    Compatible **moves from either parent** — including remembered moves outside the active moveset — can be inherited by the offspring without any held item.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Other Inheritance" icon="dna" href="/features/breeding">
    **Abilities, Poké Balls, and forms** also follow their own inheritance rules. Research the species you're breeding to take full advantage of these.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  If two Pokémon from different players breed together, agree with your friend beforehand about who collects the eggs — both of you can access the pasture, so coordination avoids any confusion.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Breeding times and special egg odds — like the chance of a rare form or hidden ability — depend on server settings and may change between seasons.
</Note>
