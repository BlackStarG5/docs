<h2>Start a Pokémon family</h2>

<p>Breeding brings compatible Pokémon together in a <strong>Pasture Block</strong> to produce eggs. It is useful for collecting species and working toward the traits you want.</p>

<h2>Your first egg</h2>

<ol><li>Place compatible Pokémon in a pasture. Sharing a pasture alone does not guarantee compatibility.</li><li>Open the pasture interface and activate the <strong>breeding button</strong>.</li><li>Wait for an egg to appear in the nesting area at the bottom, then collect it.</li><li>Keep the egg in <strong>your player inventory</strong> while its hatching timer runs. Storing it elsewhere pauses that timer.</li></ol>

<h2>Planning the next generation</h2>

<ul><li><strong>Everstone:</strong> helps pass down a parent’s nature.</li><li><strong>Destiny Knot and Power items:</strong> influence inherited IVs—the natural stats a Pokémon is born with.</li><li><strong>Egg moves:</strong> compatible moves can be inherited, including remembered moves outside the active moveset.</li><li><strong>Other traits:</strong> abilities, Poké Balls, and forms can also follow inheritance rules.</li></ul>

<p><strong>Good to know:</strong> compatible Pokémon belonging to different players can breed together. Agree with friends beforehand about who collects the eggs! Breeding times and special chances depend on server settings.</p>
