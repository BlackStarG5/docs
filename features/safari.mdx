<h2>A different kind of Pokémon hunt</h2>

<p>The Safari is a dedicated dimension with <strong>18 type-themed biomes</strong>. Explore different habitats and make the most of your visit while searching for Pokémon.</p>

<h2>Before you head in</h2>

<ul><li><strong>Bring Safari Balls:</strong> Safari catches use these special balls.</li><li><strong>Watch your time:</strong> visits use a daily time allowance, with limits set by the server.</li><li><strong>Be ready:</strong> Pokémon can flee, so an encounter does not guarantee a catch.</li></ul>

<p>Follow Hikari Haven’s current entrance instructions and check your supplies before beginning a visit.</p>

<h2>More places to discover</h2>

<p>Depending on the enabled content, the adventure system also includes:</p>

<ul><li><strong>Hoopa portals:</strong> temporary gateways to surprise destinations.</li><li><strong>Sinnoh Underground:</strong> excavation for spheres, fossils, and treasures, plus Pokémon hideaways.</li><li><strong>Distortion World:</strong> unusual exploration challenges, including parkour and mazes.</li><li><strong>Pokémon nests:</strong> wild eggs that use an Incubator.</li></ul>

<h2>Plan your next trip</h2>

<p>Check the available incense and perfume effects before using them; these can influence spawning. Locations, rewards, and access rules may vary with the server’s setup.</p>

<p>For deeper explanations of the underlying systems, visit the <a href="https://cobblesafari.maxigregrze.fr/en">official Safari wiki</a>.</p>
