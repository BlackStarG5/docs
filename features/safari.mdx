The Safari is a dedicated dimension with 18 type-themed biomes — each habitat hosts different Pokémon suited to that type. Time your visits carefully and stock up on Safari Balls before heading in, because once your daily allowance runs out you'll have to wait until the next day to continue your hunt.

## Before You Go

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring Safari Balls">
    Safari catches require **Safari Balls** — regular Poké Balls don't work inside the dimension. Stock up before you enter so you're not forced out early with an empty bag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your daily time allowance">
    Each visit draws from a daily time budget set by the server. Review how much time you have remaining before entering so you can plan which biomes to prioritise during the session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare for fleeing Pokémon">
    Pokémon inside the Safari can flee mid-encounter. You won't be able to weaken them with your own Pokémon first, so bring enough Safari Balls to account for missed attempts and fleeing targets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## More Adventures Beyond the Safari

The server's adventure system extends well beyond the Safari dimension. Depending on enabled content, you can explore these additional destinations:

  <Card title="Sinnoh Underground" icon="gem" href="/features/safari">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Distortion World" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" href="/features/safari">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Pokémon Nests" icon="egg" href="/features/safari">
