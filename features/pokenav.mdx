PokeNav helps you understand local spawn possibilities and search more deliberately — especially useful in new areas or when hunting a specific partner. Rather than wandering and hoping for the best, you can read the data, identify promising conditions, and move with purpose toward the Pokémon you want.

## Checking Local Spawns

Open the spawn info display to see which Pokémon **can appear at your current location**, the probability of each one, and the environmental conditions that influence those chances — time of day, weather, biome, and more. Scan the full list before committing to a waiting spot, then position yourself where the conditions favour the Pokémon you're after.

Appearing in the spawn list is not a guarantee. It tells you which Pokémon the game is allowed to generate in your area — not that one is already standing nearby or that one will spawn during your visit. Use the list to choose *where* to be, then give it time.

## Finding What's Already There

Once you've picked your location, these three tools help you locate individual Pokémon that have already spawned in the world:

- **Finder** — displays a directional 3D arrow that points toward a spawned Pokémon in three-dimensional space. Follow the arrow to close in on your target without circling aimlessly.
- **PokéFinder** — provides a radar-style interface that reveals nearby spawned Pokémon within a set radius. Useful when you want a wider picture of what's currently out in your surroundings.
- **FishingNav** — checks fishing-specific spawn possibilities at your current water tile, so you can identify the best fishing spots for a target species before casting.

## Getting Started with PokeNav

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look up PokeNav devices in your recipe viewer">
    Open your in-game recipe viewer (such as JEI or REI) and search for PokeNav. Review the available device types — Finder, PokéFinder, FishingNav — and choose the one that fits your current goal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Craft the device and read its tooltip">
    Gather the required materials and craft your chosen device. Before using it, read the item tooltip carefully — it describes the device's range, behaviour, and any use conditions you should know about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Options → Controls for keybindings">
    Navigate to **Options → Controls** in your Minecraft settings and search for PokeNav-related bindings. Assign comfortable keys so you can open and cycle through the interface smoothly during an active hunt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the data to plan where and when to explore">
    Combine spawn info with the tracking tools: use spawn info to find a promising area, then activate the Finder or PokéFinder once you arrive to pinpoint what's actually there. Adjust your timing based on the conditions listed for your target species.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Tracking helps you locate Pokémon that are already spawned in the world — it does not summon new ones. Addon compatibility depends on whether the target Pokémon uses Cobblemon's supported spawning system; some modded species may not appear in PokeNav's results.
</Info>
