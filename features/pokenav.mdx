<h2>Your exploration companion</h2>

<p><strong>PokeNav</strong> helps you understand local Pokémon spawns and search more deliberately. It is especially useful when you are exploring a new area or hunting a particular partner.</p>

<h2>Check the local possibilities</h2>

<p>Use the spawn information to see which Pokémon <strong>can appear at your current location</strong>, their chances, and the conditions that influence them. Read those conditions before waiting in one spot.</p>

<p><strong>Important:</strong> appearing in a spawn list does not mean a Pokémon is already nearby. It also does not guarantee that it will spawn during your visit.</p>

<h2>Find what is already there</h2>

<ul><li><strong>Finder:</strong> helps track spawned Pokémon using a directional 3D arrow.</li><li><strong>PokéFinder:</strong> provides a radar-style way to search.</li><li><strong>FishingNav:</strong> checks fishing-related spawn possibilities.</li></ul>

<h2>Getting started</h2>

<ol><li>Look up the available navigation devices in your recipe viewer.</li><li>Read the device’s tooltip and open its interface.</li><li>Check <code>Options → Controls</code> for relevant keybindings if needed.</li><li>Use the information to choose where and when to explore.</li></ol>

<p>Tracking helps you locate Pokémon; it does not summon them. Addon compatibility depends on whether those Pokémon use Cobblemon’s supported spawning system.</p>
