<h2>Your collection starts at the Hub</h2>

<p>Visit <strong>Hikari Haven’s Hub shop to buy card packs</strong>. Open boosters to discover Pokémon cards, then build a collection around your favorite species, types, or generations.</p>

<h2>Collect and organize</h2>

<ul><li><strong>Booster packs:</strong> different packs focus on particular sets, generations, or types.</li><li><strong>Card variants:</strong> look out for different rarities and special artwork, including full-art shiny versions.</li><li><strong>Binders:</strong> keep cards together and browse your collection.</li><li><strong>Card Cabinets:</strong> store larger collections with search and sorting tools.</li></ul>

<h2>More than a collection</h2>

<p>Equipping a binder in the appropriate accessory slot can provide <strong>passive bonuses</strong>. Check the displayed effects and server settings before building around them.</p>

<p>A <strong>Grading Station</strong> grades cards from 1–10 and can affect their stat multipliers. A <strong>Recycler</strong> turns unwanted duplicates into <strong>Cobblecard Dust</strong>, giving spare pulls another purpose.</p>

<h2>Discover and display</h2>

<p>The <strong>Instant-Dex</strong> system uses a Card Structure Disk and dust to scan wild Pokémon and compile cards. <strong>Holo Projectors</strong> let you display prized finds.</p>

<p><strong>Collector tip:</strong> check your binder before recycling duplicates, and use the installed recipe viewer to find the equipment available in Hikari Haven’s version.</p>
