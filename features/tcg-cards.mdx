Hikari Haven includes a full Pokémon TCG collecting system — visit the Hub shop to buy booster packs, open them for cards, and build a collection around your favorite species or generations. Different packs focus on particular sets, types, and eras, and each pull can yield cards at varying rarities, including full-art shiny versions.

## Starting Your Collection

Head to the Hub shop and pick up some booster packs to begin. From there, everything else opens up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy booster packs at the Hub shop">
    Visit the Hub shop and purchase the booster packs you want. Different packs contain different sets and generation pools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open packs to receive cards">
    Open your packs to discover what's inside. Rarer pulls include special artwork and shiny variants.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize cards into Binders or Card Cabinets">
    Store your collection in a Binder for portability or in a Card Cabinet for larger collections with search and sorting tools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the Grading Station or Recycler on duplicates">
    Grade cards to potentially improve their stat multipliers, or send unwanted duplicates through the Recycler to convert them into Cobblecard Dust for crafting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Storage and Organization

Two main tools keep your collection tidy:

- **Binders** — portable storage you can carry in your inventory and browse card by card. Great for an active collection you want on hand.
- **Card Cabinets** — designed for larger collections, with built-in search and sorting so you can find specific cards quickly without scrolling through everything.

## More Than Collecting

Cards offer benefits beyond just looking great. Use each feature to get more out of your collection.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Passive Bonuses" icon="bolt" href="/features/tcg-cards">
    Equip a Binder in the accessory slot to gain passive stat effects. Check the displayed effects and server settings to understand what's active.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Grading Station" icon="star" href="/features/tcg-cards">
    Submit cards to the Grading Station to receive a grade from 1–10. Higher grades can affect a card's stat multipliers, rewarding well-preserved pulls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recycler" icon="recycle" href="/features/tcg-cards">
    Feed duplicate cards into the Recycler to convert them into Cobblecard Dust. Use that dust in crafting recipes rather than letting spares go to waste.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Instant-Dex" icon="book-open" href="/features/tcg-cards">
    Use a Card Structure Disk and Cobblecard Dust to scan wild Pokémon in the field and compile their cards directly into your collection.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Displaying Your Collection

Once you've built up a collection you're proud of, show it off. Place **Holo Projectors** around your base to display your prized cards as glowing holographic showcases that other players can admire.

<Tip>
  Check your Binder for duplicates before recycling — you might want the extra copy for trades with other players. Recycling is permanent, so confirm you don't need the card first.
</Tip>
