<h2>Make an entrance</h2>

<p><strong>Ball Capsules</strong> decorate the moment your Pokémon leaves its Poké Ball. Mix firework effects and stickers to create a look that suits your partner—bright, dramatic, or simply cute.</p>

<h2>Create your capsule</h2>

<ol><li>Obtain a <strong>Ball Capsule</strong> and the effects you want to use.</li><li>Combine the capsule with <strong>Firework Stars</strong>. Multiple stars let you build a more elaborate effect.</li><li>Add compatible <strong>Stickers</strong> for extra themed particles.</li><li>Use a <strong>Capsule Station</strong> to equip the finished capsule to your Pokémon.</li><li>Send your Pokémon out and enjoy its new introduction!</li></ol>

<h2>Adding stickers</h2>

<p>Sticker recipes use <strong>Paper, Gunpowder, and an extra ingredient</strong> that determines the effect. Type-themed stickers use type gems. Check your installed recipe viewer for the exact recipe and available choices.</p>

<p>You can combine stickers and firework stars in the same capsule, giving you room to experiment with different themes.</p>

<h2>Style tip</h2>

<p>Try matching your effects to your Pokémon’s type, colors, or personality. Capsules change the visual send-out effect; they are a way to personalize your team rather than a battle upgrade.</p>
