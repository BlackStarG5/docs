Ball Capsules let you customize the moment your Pokémon leaves its Poké Ball — mix Firework Stars and Stickers at a Capsule Station to create a send-out effect that fits your partner's personality. Whether you want something dramatic, elegant, or playful, capsules give every member of your team a unique introduction.

## Creating Your Capsule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather your Ball Capsule and effects">
    Obtain a **Ball Capsule** along with the Firework Stars and Stickers you want to use. Knowing your intended effect before you start makes the crafting process smoother.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Combine the capsule with Firework Stars">
    Craft the capsule together with **Firework Stars**. Adding multiple stars builds a more elaborate, multi-layered effect when your Pokémon is sent out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Stickers for themed particles">
    Incorporate compatible **Stickers** to layer additional themed particles onto the effect. Stickers and Firework Stars can be used together in the same capsule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Equip the capsule at a Capsule Station">
    Bring your finished capsule to a **Capsule Station** and equip it to the Pokémon of your choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your Pokémon out">
    Enter a battle or use your Pokémon normally — enjoy the custom entrance you've created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding Stickers

Sticker recipes use **Paper, Gunpowder, and a type ingredient** that determines the visual theme. Type-themed stickers use type gems as their crafting ingredient, letting you match the effect closely to your Pokémon's type. Check your installed recipe viewer for the exact recipes and the full range of available Sticker options on Hikari Haven.

<Tip>
  Try matching your effects to your Pokémon's type, colors, or personality. You can combine Stickers and Firework Stars in the same capsule for more creative and layered themes.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Ball Capsules are purely visual — they change the send-out effect but have no impact on battle performance or stats.
</Note>
