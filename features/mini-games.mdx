<h2>A little friendly competition</h2>

<p>Arcade machines offer quick games with their own rules and rewards. Open a machine’s menu, choose an available game, and read its controls and entry cost before starting.</p>

<h2>What can you play?</h2>

<ul><li><strong>Flappy Bird:</strong> guide Butterfree through gaps.</li><li><strong>Snake:</strong> steer Onix toward berries without hitting walls or its own body.</li><li><strong>Block Stacker:</strong> time your placement to keep rows aligned.</li><li><strong>Voltorb Flip:</strong> use row and column clues to uncover multipliers while avoiding Voltorb.</li><li><strong>Other supported games:</strong> Roulette, Slot Machine, Derby, and Blackjack add more ways to play when enabled.</li></ul>

<h2>Arcade Coins and your Coin Case</h2>

<p>These games use <strong>Arcade Coins</strong>. A <strong>Coin Case</strong> stores a virtual balance and lets you deposit or withdraw physical coins. When you carry one, payouts go into the case first.</p>

<p>Check the reward shop to see what your coins can buy. Available games, costs, payouts, and shop rewards are set by the server.</p>

<h2>Play at your own pace</h2>

<p>Start with a game you enjoy and learn its rules before spending more coins. Random outcomes never guarantee a particular reward. Arcade Coins are a separate system from CobbleDollars and the currencies used by other machines.</p>
