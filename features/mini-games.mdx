The arcade offers quick mini-games with their own rules and coin rewards — open a machine, choose a game, read the entry cost and controls, then play. Each game rewards Arcade Coins on success, which you can collect and save up for the reward shop.

## Available Games

Every arcade machine has its own gameplay style. Pick the one that suits you and learn the rules before spending coins on entry.

Additional games are available when enabled by the server, including **Roulette**, **Slot Machine**, **Derby**, and **Blackjack** — each with its own rules and payout structure.

## Arcade Coins and the Coin Case

All arcade games use **Arcade Coins** as their currency. A **Coin Case** stores a virtual balance so you don't have to carry physical coins in your inventory — when you have one, payouts go directly into your case.

Spend accumulated coins at the **reward shop** to exchange them for prizes. Check the shop to see what's currently available and plan which games to focus on.

<Note>
  Available games, entry costs, payouts, and reward shop items are determined by the server and may change over time. Arcade Coins are completely separate from CobbleDollars and other machine currencies — they cannot be exchanged or substituted for one another.
</Note>
