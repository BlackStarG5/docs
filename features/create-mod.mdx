Create adds mechanical tools and animated contraptions for building, processing resources, and automating everyday tasks — start small with one useful machine, then grow your workshop as you learn the systems. Whether you want a simple sorting setup or a sprawling automated factory, Create gives you the tools to make it move.

## Learning the Basics

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a Create component">
    Locate a Create component in your inventory or browse for it in the installed recipe viewer. Familiarizing yourself with the available parts early saves time when you're ready to build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the Ponder demonstration">
    Look for the **Ponder** prompt on the component. These in-game demos show exactly how each supported piece works and how it connects to other parts of the system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check materials and processing steps">
    Open the recipe viewer to see what materials you need and how processing sequences are structured before you start gathering resources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a small test setup first">
    Assemble a compact test rig before wiring it into a larger project. This makes it much easier to spot problems and adjust the design without untangling a full build.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ideas for Your First Workshop

You don't need a grand plan to get started — pick one useful idea and build outward from there:

- A resource-processing corner beside your storage room
- An automated production line for something you use regularly
- A moving feature that gives your home, shop, or town some character

<Tip>
  A compact, understandable workshop is far easier to expand and troubleshoot than a sprawling one built all at once. Grow one section at a time.
</Tip>

## Create Addons

Addons extend what Create can do by adding new machines, materials, and building options. To see what's actually available on Hikari Haven, browse items and recipes directly in the server's installed modpack — recipes and components from a different pack version may not match what you find in-game.

<Warning>
  Keep your machines within your own claimed space and follow server rules for automation. Contraptions that affect other players' areas or put excessive load on the server may be removed.
</Warning>
