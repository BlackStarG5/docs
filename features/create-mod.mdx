<h2>Build something that moves</h2>

<p><strong>Create</strong> adds mechanical tools and animated contraptions for building, processing resources, and automating everyday jobs. Start with one useful machine, then grow your workshop as you learn.</p>

<h2>Learn by watching</h2>

<ol><li>Find a Create component in your inventory or the installed recipe viewer.</li><li>Look for its <strong>Ponder</strong> prompt. These in-game demonstrations show how supported components work and fit together.</li><li>Check the recipe viewer for the materials and processing steps you need.</li><li>Build a small test setup before connecting it to a larger project.</li></ol>

<h2>Ideas for your first workshop</h2>

<ul><li>A resource-processing corner beside your storage room.</li><li>An automated production line for something you regularly use.</li><li>A moving feature that gives your home, shop, or town some character.</li></ul>

<h2>Exploring addons</h2>

<p>Create addons can expand the available machines, materials, and building options. Browse the items and recipes in <strong>Hikari Haven’s installed pack</strong> to see what is actually available; recipes from a different pack may not match.</p>

<p><strong>Build thoughtfully:</strong> keep your machines within your own space and follow server rules for automation. A compact, understandable workshop is easier to expand and troubleshoot.</p>
