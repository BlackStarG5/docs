Hikari Haven supports anime-style battle gimmicks — Mega Evolution, Z-Moves, and Terastallization — each requiring the right equipment and a compatible Pokémon. Prepare your trainer loadout and your partner's held item before stepping onto the battlefield, or you won't be able to activate the power when it counts.

## The Three Main Gimmicks

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mega Evolution">
    Mega Evolution temporarily transforms a compatible Pokémon into a stronger form for the duration of a battle. To trigger it you need two things: the **Key Stone** equipped as a trainer accessory, and the **species-specific Mega Stone** held by your Pokémon. One Mega Stone does not work for every Pokémon — check the item tooltip to confirm which species it supports before heading into battle.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Z-Moves">
    Z-Moves channel a Pokémon's energy into a single, devastating attack. Equip a **Z-Ring** as your trainer accessory and give your Pokémon a compatible **Z-Crystal** to hold. When the conditions are met — the right Pokémon, the right crystal, and the right move in the moveset — the Z-Move option appears in battle. Each Z-Crystal is tied to a specific type or species, so match the crystal to your battle plan.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Terastallization">
    Terastallization shifts a Pokémon's type for the rest of the battle, letting you flip weaknesses or amplify strengths at a pivotal moment. You need a **Tera Orb** as your trainer accessory. Your Pokémon's **Tera Type** determines what type it becomes after Terastallizing — check it in advance so you can factor it into your strategy.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to Prepare

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check item compatibility">
    Read the transformation item's tooltip and confirm which Pokémon it supports. A mismatched item will do nothing in battle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Equip your trainer accessories">
    Open your accessory slots and equip the gimmick item — Key Stone, Z-Ring, or Tera Orb — that matches the gimmick you plan to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your Pokémon the required held item">
    Assign the appropriate Mega Stone or Z-Crystal to your Pokémon's held item slot. Terastallization relies on the Pokémon's inherent Tera Type, so no separate held item is needed beyond the Tera Orb on your trainer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter battle and activate the gimmick">
    Start the battle normally. Before selecting your move, look for the gimmick activation button that appears when all conditions are satisfied. Trigger it, then choose your move to unleash the full effect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Finding Gimmick Items

You don't need to trade or craft to get started — gimmick items appear in loot chests scattered throughout the world. Explore dungeons, structures, and wilderness areas to turn up Mega Stones, Z-Crystals, and accessory components. Personal loot chests tied to your progress can also reward transformation items, so check them regularly as you advance.

<Info>
  Other mechanics like Dynamax, Gigantamax, and legendary transformations may also be available depending on the server's installed version. Follow event rules before using any gimmick competitively.
</Info>
