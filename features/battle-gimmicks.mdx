<h2>Power up your battle plan</h2>

<p>Hikari Haven features <strong>Mega Evolution, Z-Moves, and Terastallization</strong>. Each mechanic needs the right equipment and a compatible Pokémon, so prepare before challenging another trainer.</p>

<h2>The main gimmicks</h2>

<ul><li><strong>Mega Evolution:</strong> pair your trainer’s Key Stone accessory with the correct Mega Stone held by an eligible Pokémon. A species-specific stone matters—one stone does not work on every partner.</li><li><strong>Z-Moves:</strong> use a Z-Ring and a suitable Z-Crystal to unlock a special move for a compatible Pokémon and moveset.</li><li><strong>Terastallization:</strong> uses a Tera Orb and your Pokémon’s Tera Type to change how you approach type matchups.</li></ul>

<h2>Getting ready</h2>

<ol><li>Read the transformation item’s tooltip and check which Pokémon it supports.</li><li>Equip trainer accessories in their appropriate slots and give your Pokémon its required held item.</li><li>Enter battle and look for the available gimmick control before choosing your move.</li></ol>

<p><strong>Explore for upgrades:</strong> Hikari Haven’s transformation items can be found through loot, including personal loot chests.</p>

<p>The mod also supports mechanics such as Dynamax, Gigantamax, and certain legendary transformations. Their availability depends on the server’s installed version and setup; follow event rules before using any gimmick competitively.</p>
