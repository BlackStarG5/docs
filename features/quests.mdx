Quests give your adventure direction — complete objectives like catching specific Pokémon, defeating wild battles, or evolving a partner to earn rewards. The system connects Cobblemon activities to FTB Quests, giving you a structured path to follow at your own pace without dictating every step of your journey.

## Following a Quest

Open the quest menu and work through each objective in order. Some quests unlock only after you've finished earlier ones, so read each quest's requirements before diving in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the quest book or menu">
    Press the keybinding assigned to the quest book or quest menu. If you're not sure which key it is, check **Options → Controls** to find it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a chapter and select a quest">
    Browse the available chapters and pick a quest you want to pursue. Each chapter groups related objectives together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read every task and prerequisite">
    Before starting, review all the listed tasks and any prerequisites. Some quests are locked until earlier objectives are complete, so knowing what's required saves time later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the listed activities">
    Carry out each task in the quest — catch the specified Pokémon, win battles, level up a partner, or whatever the objective asks — and watch your progress update in the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your rewards">
    Once all tasks are finished, return to the quest menu and claim your rewards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Types of Quest Objectives

Quests on Hikari Haven can ask you to do a variety of things. Expect to encounter objectives like:

- Catch specified Pokémon
- Defeat or knock out wild Pokémon
- Train a Pokémon to a target level
- Evolve a partner
- Challenge supported NPC trainers

<Tip>
  Some quests are locked until earlier objectives are completed. If something isn't showing as available, check the quest menu for prerequisites you may need to finish first.
</Tip>

<Note>
  If your progress seems stuck, verify the exact task wording and check whether any prerequisites are still locked. If the issue persists after double-checking, share the quest name and a screenshot with staff so they can investigate.
</Note>
