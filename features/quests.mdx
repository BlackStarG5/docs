<h2>A little direction for your adventure</h2>

<p>Quests turn activities such as catching, battling, training, and evolving Pokémon into goals you can follow. The quest system connects Cobblemon activities to <strong>FTB Quests</strong>.</p>

<h2>Following a quest</h2>

<ol><li>Open the <strong>quest book or quest menu</strong>. Check <code>Options → Controls</code> for its keybinding if you cannot find it.</li><li>Choose an available chapter and select a quest.</li><li>Read every task and prerequisite before starting. Some quests unlock only after earlier objectives are complete.</li><li>Carry out the listed activities and check your progress in the menu.</li><li>Claim any available rewards once the quest is complete.</li></ol>

<h2>Possible objectives</h2>

<ul><li>Catch specified Pokémon.</li><li>Defeat or knock out wild Pokémon.</li><li>Train Pokémon to a target level.</li><li>Evolve a partner.</li><li>Challenge supported NPC trainers.</li></ul>

<h2>Read the quest details</h2>

<p>Chapters, targets, and rewards are created for the server or modpack; the quest integration does not supply a complete adventure by itself. The quests visible in your menu are the ones currently available.</p>

<p><strong>Stuck?</strong> check the exact task wording and any locked prerequisites first. If the objective still will not progress, share the quest name and a screenshot with staff.</p>
