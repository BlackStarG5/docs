Raid Dens are multiplayer encounters where a group of trainers battles a powerful Pokémon with shared HP — every participant's damage contributes to bringing it down together. Raid crystals mark these encounters across the world and come in different tiers, so higher-tier dens call for more preparation and a coordinated team.

## Starting a Raid

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a raid crystal and review the encounter">
    Locate a raid crystal in the world and examine it to learn the boss Pokémon's type, tier, and any relevant details. Use this information to assemble the right team before committing to a run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The first player enters and becomes the host">
    Whoever enters the raid crystal first takes the host role. As the host, you control whether other players are allowed to join the session, so step in only when your group is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Other players request to join">
    Your teammates interact with the same crystal to send a join request. Accept them from the raid interface — you can accept or deny each request while the raid is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your team and coordinate">
    Before the battle begins, confirm your final team and communicate your roles. Decide who focuses on the boss's weaknesses, who provides support, and how you'll handle status conditions together.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the Battle Works

Each participating trainer fights in their own **separate, simultaneous battle** — you won't be sharing a battle screen with your teammates. Despite the separate instances, the boss has a single pool of **shared HP** that all trainers deplete together. Every hit lands on the same target, so even a trainer with a lower-level team still contributes meaningfully to the group's progress.

<Warning>
  Once all of your Pokémon have fainted, you cannot rejoin the fight. You can still receive rewards if your remaining teammates successfully complete the raid, so stay engaged and cheer them on.
</Warning>

## After Victory

A successful raid rewards everyone who participated. You receive the raid Pokémon caught in your chosen Poké Ball, and a **Raid Pouch** containing a random selection of loot. Available encounter tiers and bonus rewards depend on the server's current setup, so keep an eye on announcements for featured raids and seasonal bonuses.

<Tip>
  Heal your entire team before entering, look up the boss's type matchups in advance, and agree on a strategy with your group — a small amount of coordination before the battle starts can make the difference between victory and a failed attempt.
</Tip>
