<h2>A challenge worth sharing</h2>

<p><strong>Raid crystals</strong> lead to encounters with powerful Pokémon in a separate raid dimension. Different tiers offer different challenges, so prepare your team before joining.</p>

<h2>Joining a raid</h2>

<ol><li>Find a raid crystal and review the encounter.</li><li>The first player to enter becomes the <strong>host</strong>.</li><li>Other players request to join; the host can accept or deny requests while the raid is active.</li><li>Choose your team carefully and coordinate with the other trainers.</li></ol>

<h2>How the battle works</h2>

<p>Players fight in separate, simultaneous battles, but the boss has <strong>shared HP</strong>. Your damage contributes to the group’s progress, so every participating trainer can help bring it down.</p>

<p><strong>Make your attempt count:</strong> once your Pokémon have fainted, you cannot rejoin the fight. You can still receive rewards if the remaining players complete the raid.</p>

<h2>After a victory</h2>

<p>A successful raid awards the raid Pokémon in your chosen ball and a <strong>Raid Pouch</strong> containing random loot. Available encounters, tiers, and additional rewards depend on the server’s setup.</p>

<p><strong>Trainer tip:</strong> heal beforehand, consider the boss’s type matchups, and agree on a plan with your group before entering.</p>
