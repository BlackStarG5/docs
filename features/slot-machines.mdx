Hikari Haven's capsule machines use a gacha-style system — spend the required in-game currency to receive a random capsule, then open it for a reward. This works differently from the reel-based Slot Machine game in the arcade. All currencies are earned through gameplay, so every spin is funded by your progress as a trainer rather than a purchase.

## Using a Capsule Machine

Each machine has its own accepted currency and capsule pool. Read the machine's display before committing anything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the machine's instructions and accepted currency">
    Examine the machine before using it. Note which currency it requires — different machines accept Relic Coins, Koban Coins, or other currencies, and they are not interchangeable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make sure you have enough">
    Confirm you hold the required amount before inserting anything. Running short mid-use can cost you the spend without a reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect your capsule">
    Once the machine processes your currency, collect the capsule that comes out. Capsule tiers include Poké, Great, Ultra, Master, and Cherish, each with its own reward pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the capsule to see your result">
    Open the capsule to reveal what's inside. Check the item before deciding whether to try again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's Inside?

The standard capsule system runs on **Relic Coins** and spans five tiers: **Poké**, **Great**, **Ultra**, **Master**, and **Cherish**. Higher tiers generally contain rarer or more valuable rewards. Possible rewards across the system include:

- Fossils
- Vitamins
- Evolution stones
- Held items
- Apricorns

## Special Machines

When the **Rocket Prize Master** is available, you can spend a **Koban Coin** to receive a Rocket Ball containing a random Pokémon. This machine operates independently from the standard Relic Coin capsules, so check its display for the current cost and available Pokémon pool before using it.

<Note>
  Hikari Haven is free-to-play — all capsule currencies are earned through gameplay, not purchased with real money. Play at a pace that feels comfortable and enjoy the rewards as a product of your in-game effort.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Results are random. A specific prize is never guaranteed, regardless of how many capsules you open. Always read the machine's displayed cost and reward information before spending your currency.
</Warning>
