<h2>Make a place your own</h2>

<p>Land claims reserve <strong>chunks</strong> of the world and apply protection settings to them. Use claims around your home, storage, and projects, then decide which friends should have access.</p>

<h2>Claiming land</h2>

<ol><li>Open the <strong>Open Parties and Claims</strong> menu using its keybinding. Find or change it in <code>Options → Controls</code>.</li><li>Use the claim interface to select available chunks within your allowance.</li><li>With the supported Xaero’s World Map integration, you can <strong>right-click</strong> a chunk or right-drag a selection to access claim actions.</li><li>Check the map boundaries and review your protection settings.</li></ol>

<h2>Sharing with friends</h2>

<p>Parties and access settings let you cooperate without opening everything to strangers. Read each permission before enabling it, especially interactions involving blocks, containers, and entities. Greyed-out settings are controlled by the server.</p>

<h2>Useful menu commands</h2>

<p>Newer versions use <code>/oclaims</code> for claims, <code>/oparties</code> for parties, and <code>/opac</code> for player configuration. Use chat autocomplete to see the options available in the installed version.</p>

<p><strong>Remember:</strong> claim limits and chunk loading depend on server settings. Claims do not replace server rules—respect other players’ builds, including unclaimed ones.</p>
