Land claims reserve chunks of the world and apply protection settings to them — use them around your home, storage, and active projects, then decide which friends can access your space. A well-placed claim keeps your builds and Pokémon safe while still letting you collaborate with the people you trust.

## Claiming Your Land

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Open Parties and Claims menu">
    Press the keybinding assigned to **Open Parties and Claims**. If you haven't set one yet, find or change it under **Options → Controls**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select chunks within your allowance">
    Use the claim interface to choose which chunks you want to protect. You can only claim up to your current allowance, so prioritize the areas you use most.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Xaero's World Map for quick claiming">
    With the Xaero's World Map integration, **right-click** a chunk on the map to access claim actions, or **right-drag** across multiple chunks to select a region at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your protection settings">
    After claiming, check the map boundaries and confirm your protection settings are configured the way you want before building further.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sharing With Friends

Parties let you cooperate with other players without opening everything to strangers. When you add someone to your party and grant them permissions, they can interact with your claimed space according to the rules you set. Read each permission carefully before enabling it — especially any that affect blocks, containers, or entities. Greyed-out settings are controlled by the server and cannot be changed by individual players.

## Claim Commands

Use the following commands to manage your claims, parties, and personal configuration. Type any command in chat and use autocomplete to explore the available sub-options for the version installed on the server.

```
/oclaims   — manage your land claims
/oparties  — manage your party and members
/opac      — player configuration settings
```

<Note>
  Claim limits and chunk loading behaviour depend on server settings and may change over time.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Claims do not replace server rules — respect other players' builds, including unclaimed ones.
</Warning>
