## More reasons to explore

**49 habitats, 10 new ruins, and the Magma Shipwreck Cove** expand the world. Habitats blend into their surroundings and provide homes for particular Pokémon; some encounters change from day to day.

<Note>
  Keep habitats intact: their disguised Habitat Blocks control local spawning. Destroying those blocks stops their Pokémon from appearing. Mark interesting places and return later to see what is around.
</Note>

## Two big changes for your team

<Columns cols={2}>
  <div>
  <h3>👑 Meet Alpha Pokémon</h3>
  <p>Watch for oversized Pokémon with glowing red eyes. These rare wild bosses scale above your team’s strongest partner, gain battle boosts, and carry TM moves.</p>
  <p>Naturally appearing Alphas lead herds of earlier-stage Pokémon. Prepare before approaching: they can defend themselves outside battle, too.</p>
  <p>Defeating them can reward training candies, Type Gems, and berries. Finding one is a chance to test your team—or catch a striking new companion.</p>
</div>

  <div>
  <h3>💿 Build your moveset</h3>
  <p>TMs teach compatible Pokémon new moves. Unlock recipes by obtaining a Pokémon that knows the move or interacting with a Data Monitor holding its TM.</p>
  <p>At a <strong>TM Machine</strong>, choose a recipe and supply a Blank TM, the matching Type Gem, and any extra ingredients shown.</p>
  <p>TMs are normally consumed when used. Check compatibility first; an incompatible Pokémon will not consume the disc. You can also discover TMs while exploring ruins.</p>
</div>
</Columns>

## New riding partners

Crobat, Skarmory, Milotic, Archeops, Goodra, Hisuian Goodra, Drampa, Duraludon, Archaludon, and Pidgeot gain riding support.

## Smaller changes worth knowing

- Pokémon now vary naturally in size, and the **Move Dex** helps you plan movesets.
- Porygon gains a Data Monitor acquisition method.
- Hover mounts lose their mining-speed penalty.
- Create gains additional Cobblemon processing recipes.
- Many models and animations are refreshed.
- **Advancements reset except starter selection** as part of the update’s cleanup.
