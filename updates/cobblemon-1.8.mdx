Cobblemon 1.8 — the **Make Your Move** update — is one of the biggest additions to the mod yet. Thirty-seven new Pokémon and four regional forms join the world, Alpha Pokémon arrive as fearsome wild bosses, and a full TM crafting system gives you new ways to fine-tune your team's movesets. On top of that, 49 new habitats, 10 ruins, the Magma Shipwreck Cove, and a fresh set of rideable Pokémon make the world richer and more rewarding to explore than ever before.

## Alpha Pokémon

Alpha Pokémon are rare, powerful wild bosses that stand apart from ordinary encounters the moment you spot them. They appear **oversized** compared to their regular counterparts and have distinctive **glowing red eyes**, making them easy to identify from a distance. Alphas spawn at a strength level above your team's strongest partner, gain extra battle boosts, and can carry TM moves that their normal counterparts don't typically know.

In the wild, Alphas naturally lead herds of earlier-stage Pokémon, so stumbling into one can mean more than a single tough fight. Be aware that they can also defend themselves *outside of battle*, so approach prepared rather than on impulse.

Defeating an Alpha Pokémon can reward you with **training candies**, **Type Gems**, and **berries** — all useful for strengthening your existing team. Beyond the rewards, finding an Alpha is a genuine test of your roster, and catching one gives you a striking, hard-won companion.

<Warning>
  Alpha Pokémon scale above your team's highest-level partner and can act aggressively outside of battle. Make sure your team is well-healed and stocked with supplies before engaging one.
</Warning>

## TMs and Movesets

TMs (Technical Machines) let you teach compatible Pokémon new moves, opening up more options when building your perfect team. Before you can craft a TM, you need to **unlock its recipe** — you do this either by having a Pokémon in your party that already knows the move, or by interacting with a **Data Monitor** while it holds the corresponding TM item.

Once you have a recipe unlocked, head to a **TM Machine** to craft it. Every TM requires three ingredients:

- A **Blank TM** as the base disc
- The **Type Gem** that matches the move's type
- Any **additional ingredients** shown in the recipe for that specific move

<Tip>
  Always check move compatibility before crafting. If the target Pokémon cannot learn the move, the TM will not be consumed — but it's still worth confirming ahead of time to save resources.
</Tip>

Keep in mind that TMs are **consumed on use** in most cases. You can also find TMs by exploring ruins, so keep an eye out while adventuring.

## New Riding Partners

Ten Pokémon gain official riding support in the 1.8 update, giving you more options for getting around the world in style. The new rideable Pokémon are:

**Crobat, Skarmory, Milotic, Archeops, Goodra, Hisuian Goodra, Drampa, Duraludon, Archaludon,** and **Pidgeot.**

As an additional quality-of-life improvement, hover mounts no longer apply a mining-speed penalty while you're riding them.

## More to Explore

The world becomes significantly larger in 1.8 with a major expansion to Pokémon habitats and new structures to discover.

**49 habitats** are added across the update, each blending naturally into the surrounding terrain and providing a home for specific Pokémon. Some habitat encounters change from day to day, so returning to a favourite spot at different times can surface different Pokémon. Ten new **ruins** are scattered throughout the world and make great places to hunt for TMs while exploring.

The standout new structure is the **Magma Shipwreck Cove** — a dramatic location that rewards trainers willing to seek it out.

<Note>
  Habitats rely on disguised **Habitat Blocks** to control local Pokémon spawning. Destroying these blocks stops their Pokémon from appearing permanently in that area. Mark interesting locations and leave the terrain intact so the spawns keep working for everyone.
</Note>

## Other Changes

<Accordion title="Natural Size Variation">
  Pokémon now vary naturally in size when they spawn in the wild, adding visual variety to every encounter and making some individuals feel genuinely unique.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Move Dex">
  The new **Move Dex** is an in-game reference tool that lets you look up move details and plan out movesets before committing to a TM or breeding path.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Porygon & Data Monitor">
  Porygon gains a new way to be obtained through the **Data Monitor** mechanic. Interact with the Data Monitor as part of your exploration to learn more.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Model & Animation Refreshes">
  Many existing Pokémon receive updated models and refreshed animations in this release, improving their look and feel across the board.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Create Mod Integration">
  The Create mod gains additional Cobblemon-compatible processing recipes, tightening the link between the two mods for players who enjoy technical builds.
</Accordion>

<Warning>
  **Advancements have been reset as part of the 1.8 update cleanup** — the only exception is your starter selection, which is preserved. You'll need to re-earn your other advancements going forward.
</Warning>
