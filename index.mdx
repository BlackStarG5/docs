Hikari Haven is a free-to-play Cobblemon SMP set in a Pokémon anime-inspired world. Connect at **play.hikari-haven.com**, install the modpack, and step into a living server full of trainers, wild Pokémon, hidden loot, and cozy places to call home. Whether you are here to chase legendaries, perfect your competitive team, or build something beautiful with friends, there is a place for you here.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the Modpack">
    Download the Modrinth App, sign in with your Minecraft account, and search for **Hikari Haven Cobblemon** to install the modpack. Allocate at least 6 GB of RAM before launching.

    [Installation guide →](/getting-started/installation)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the Server">
    Launch the modpack and connect to **play.hikari-haven.com**. You will arrive at Spawn — take a moment to explore the town center and get your bearings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Rules">
    Familiarize yourself with the server rules before heading out. They cover claims, battles, trading, and community expectations.

    [Server Rules →](/getting-started/server-rules)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Home Realm">
    Pick a building realm — **White Forest** is open to all new players. Set a home with `/sethome` and start your adventure.

    [The Different Realms →](/getting-started/realms)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Server IP:** `play.hikari-haven.com` — Free to play. No crates. No keys. Just a world worth exploring.
</Note>
