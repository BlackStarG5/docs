Hikari Haven uses a custom modpack to deliver the full Cobblemon experience. The easiest way to get it running is through the **Modrinth App**, which automatically downloads, installs, and manages every required mod for you — no manual file juggling needed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Modrinth App">
    Head to [modrinth.com/app](https://modrinth.com/app) and click the **Download** button. Run the downloaded installer and follow the on-screen steps to complete the installation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign In">
    Open the Modrinth App and sign in with your **Minecraft account**. You must have a valid, purchased Minecraft: Java Edition licence to play.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the Modpack">
    Click the **compass icon** in the left sidebar to open the modpack browser. Search for **Hikari Haven Cobblemon**, then click the **+ Install** button next to it. The app downloads and sets up all required mods automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch">
    Once installation finishes, hit the **Play** button on the Hikari Haven Cobblemon modpack tile. Minecraft will launch with all mods loaded and ready to go.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Allocate **at least 6 GB of RAM** to avoid performance issues with this modpack. To adjust your memory allocation, go to **Settings → Synced Settings → Java and Memory** inside the Modrinth App.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Once you are in-game, open the multiplayer menu and connect to **play.hikari-haven.com** to join the server. Welcome to Hikari Haven!
</Note>
