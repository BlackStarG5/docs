## Welcome to the neighborhood

Your adventure begins in a hand-built town full of little details to discover. Wander the streets, get familiar with your surroundings, and enjoy a first look at life in Hikari Haven.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Explore the town center" icon="store">
    The mall sits at the heart of Spawn and makes a handy landmark while you learn your way around. Start there, then branch out along the surrounding streets. You do not need to rush straight into the wilderness.
  </Card>

  <Card title="A town full of life" icon="users">
    Look out for Pokémon roaming naturally and other players hanging out and having fun. Say hello, bring a friend for a stroll, or ask the community for guidance. Spawn is a place to meet fellow trainers as well as the start of your adventure.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Before you head out

- **Read the [Server Rules](/getting-started/server-rules).** Learn the expectations for claims, battles, trading, and shared spaces.
- **Respect the town.** Leave builds and decorations as you found them, keep paths clear, and ask before using someone else’s belongings.
- **Explore at your own pace.** Get your bearings, meet a few neighbors, and decide where to head next.

<Warning>
  See someone breaking the rules? **Do not hesitate to report it to staff.** Share their name, location, and screenshots or video if you can. Report privately through our [Discord](https://discord.gg/TRGejrJNwf), and let staff handle it rather than arguing or retaliating.
</Warning>
