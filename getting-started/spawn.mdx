Your adventure in Hikari Haven begins at **Spawn** — a hand-crafted town packed with detail, personality, and a steady flow of fellow trainers. Take your time soaking it all in before you venture into the wider world. There is no rush, and the town itself is well worth exploring.

## The Town Center

The **mall** sits at the heart of Spawn and serves as your go-to landmark while you are still learning the layout. Start there, then branch out along the surrounding streets and alleyways. Admire the builds, peek into shops, and get a feel for the community that has shaped this place. You do not need to charge straight into the wilderness on your first day — there is plenty of atmosphere to enjoy right here in town.

## Life Around Every Corner

Keep your eyes open as you explore. **Pokémon roam naturally** throughout Spawn and the surrounding area, making every stroll feel alive. You will also find other players hanging out, chatting, and going about their own adventures. Say hello, ask for a tour, or simply watch the world go by — Spawn is as much a meeting place as it is a starting point.

## Before You Head Out

When you feel ready to explore beyond Spawn, make sure you have ticked off these essentials first:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the Server Rules">
    Familiarise yourself with the expectations for claims, battles, trading, and shared spaces. Use `/rules` in-game or check the Hikari Haven Discord.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Respect Other Players' Builds and Paths">
    Leave decorations and builds exactly as you found them. Keep paths clear, and always ask before touching anything that belongs to another player.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore at Your Own Pace">
    Get your bearings, meet a few neighbours, and decide what you want your first adventure to look like. There is no timer — enjoy the journey.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you see someone breaking the rules, **report it to staff** — do not argue or retaliate. Head to the Hikari Haven Discord and share the player's name, their location, and any screenshots or video evidence you have. Let staff handle the situation.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Type `/spawn` from anywhere in the world to instantly return to the town center whenever you need your bearings.
</Tip>
