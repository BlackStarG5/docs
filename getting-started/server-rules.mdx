Reading and following the rules is what keeps Hikari Haven a welcoming, enjoyable place for every trainer. Take a few minutes to go through this page before your first session — understanding the expectations from the start makes everything more fun for you and everyone around you.

## General Conduct

Hikari Haven is a community server, and how you treat other players shapes the experience for everyone.

- **Be respectful.** Treat every player and staff member with courtesy, whether in chat, voice, or Discord. Harassment, hate speech, slurs, and personal attacks are never acceptable.
- **No griefing.** Do not destroy, steal from, or deliberately damage another player's builds, farms, or Pokémon setups — claimed or otherwise.
- **No cheating or exploiting.** Using hacks, unauthorised mods, duplication glitches, or any other exploit that gives you an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited. If you discover a bug, report it to staff instead of abusing it.
- **Keep chat clean.** Avoid excessive caps, spam, and inappropriate topics. Follow the same standards in private messages as you do in public chat.

## Claims & Building

Protecting your space — and respecting everyone else's — keeps the building realms a great place to live.

- **Claim your land before you build.** Unclaimed builds may not receive protection. Use the appropriate claiming tools as soon as you start a new project.
- **Respect neighbouring claims.** Do not build directly on top of, or excessively close to, another player's claim without their permission. Give people space.
- **Leave unclaimed builds alone.** Just because land is unclaimed does not mean it is free to take or destroy. If something looks like it belongs to someone, treat it that way.
- **No lag machines or oversized farms.** Builds that deliberately harm server performance affect everyone. Keep redstone contraptions and mob farms reasonably sized.

## Battling & Trading

Pokémon battles and player trading are core parts of the Hikari Haven experience — keep them fair and fun.

- **Play fair in battles.** Agree on the format and any house rules before a match begins. Do not disconnect mid-battle to avoid a loss.
- **Agree on trade terms before confirming.** Review every trade window carefully before accepting. Once a trade is complete, it cannot be reversed.
- **Do not scam.** Misrepresenting a Pokémon's stats, moves, or nature to manipulate a trade is considered scamming and will be treated as a rule violation.
- **No unsolicited PvP.** Do not attack other players without their consent in areas where PvP is not explicitly enabled.

## Reporting Issues

If something goes wrong, bring it to the staff team — they are here to help.

- Head to the **Hikari Haven Discord** and open a report or ticket.
- Include the **offending player's name**, your **approximate location** at the time, and any **screenshots or video evidence** you have.
- Do not air disputes in public chat. Keep reports private so staff can investigate fairly.
- Let staff handle the situation. Do not retaliate, grief back, or try to resolve rule violations yourself.

<Note>
  The full, official rules document is always available in-game via `/rules` and pinned in the Hikari Haven Discord server. When in doubt, check there for the most up-to-date version.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Breaking the rules may result in a **verbal warning**, a **temporary ban**, or a **permanent removal** from the server, depending on the severity and frequency of the violation. Ignorance of the rules is not an excuse — read them before you play.
</Warning>
