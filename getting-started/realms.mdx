Hikari Haven is divided into two types of worlds: **Building Realms**, where you settle down, construct a home, and put down permanent roots, and **Resource Worlds**, where you venture out to gather materials for your next big project. Your items and Pokémon travel with you between every realm, so you never lose your progress — but keep in mind that your in-game money balance is tracked separately in each realm.

## Building Realms

Building Realms are permanent, protected spaces designed for long-term settlement. Claim your land, build your base, and make it your own — just be sure to respect your neighbours and follow the land-claiming rules.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Black City" icon="city" href="/getting-started/realms">
    A building realm reserved for **legacy (whitelisted) players**. If you have been granted access, travel there with `/server blackcity`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="White Forest" icon="tree" href="/getting-started/realms">
    The main building realm, **open to all players**. Find your plot, stake your claim, and start building with `/server whiteforest`.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Resource Worlds

Resource Worlds are designed for exploration and gathering. Head there to mine, loot, and collect materials — but do not set up a permanent home in these worlds, as they may reset periodically.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="The Wild" icon="map" href="/getting-started/realms">
    The overworld resource world. Gather wood, stone, and natural materials. Travel there with `/server thewild`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The End" icon="moon" href="/getting-started/realms">
    Hunt for End-exclusive resources and take on the Ender Dragon. Jump in with `/end`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The Nether" icon="fire" href="/getting-started/realms">
    Collect Nether-specific materials like Netherite and Blaze Rods. Travel there with `/nether`.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Your **items and Pokémon carry over** between all realms — you will never arrive empty-handed. However, your **money balance does not transfer**: each realm maintains its own separate economy, so check your `/balance` after switching worlds.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Run `/sethome` to save your base location before jumping into a resource world. That way you can always `/home` straight back when you are done gathering.
</Tip>
