title: "Modrinth"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Modrinth App">
    Download the launcher from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/app), then run the installer and follow the setup steps.

    ![Modrinth App download page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BlackStarG5/hikari-haven-wiki/main/.gitbook/assets/image.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the launcher">
    Open Modrinth App and sign in with your Minecraft account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the modpack">
    Open the compass tab and search for **Hikari Haven Cobblemon**, then select **Install**. You can also open our [modpack page](https://modrinth.com/modpack/treys-cobblemon-server).

    ![Find the modpack browser in Modrinth App](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BlackStarG5/hikari-haven-wiki/main/.gitbook/assets/image%20\(1\).png)

    ![Install Hikari Haven Cobblemon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BlackStarG5/hikari-haven-wiki/main/.gitbook/assets/image%20\(2\).png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch and join">
    Press **Play** once installation finishes. In Minecraft, open Multiplayer and connect to **play.hikari-haven.com**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  We recommend at least **6 GB of allocated RAM** for this modpack. Adjust it in **Settings → Synced settings → Java and Memory**.
</Warning>
