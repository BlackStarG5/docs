Welcome to **Hikari Haven** — a Pokémon anime-inspired Cobblemon SMP where you can explore vibrant landscapes, build your dream team, and settle into a cozy community that's worth calling home. Whether you're chasing down your first legendary, setting up a base with friends, or browsing the marketplace for a rare trade, Hikari Haven has a place for every kind of trainer.

## What Makes Hikari Haven Different

Hikari Haven is built around one simple idea: the adventure should always come first. There are no crates, no keys, and no paywalls standing between you and your next discovery — just a **free-to-play** world that rewards exploration and dedication. The server runs the [Cobblemon mod](https://cobblemon.com), bringing Pokémon into Minecraft with faithful animations, real battle mechanics, and a growing Pokédex that keeps expanding. The whole experience is wrapped in the warm, adventurous spirit of the **Pokémon anime**, so every route feels like it could hide something exciting. On top of all that, the community is kept intentionally cozy — this is an SMP, which means real players building, battling, and trading alongside you every day.
